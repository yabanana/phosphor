# F15 plan — adversarial review (2026-11-13)

Base: `189b4bb`. Commits ac92a1e/e6d45cf/189b4bb (refraction always traced, coat/thin-glass emitter ownership, GI revision, reserved feature constant) were taken into account and are not re-reported.

## Blockers

**B1. The base model is neither glTF Appendix B nor furnace-bounded, so ticket 03 fails on unchanged code.** Direct diffuse is `(1−F(mix(0.04,albedo,m)))·(1−m)` (`shaders/material_shading.h:85,241`, `restir_common.h:140`). Appendix B uses `(1−m)·(1−F(0.04))`, a 2× gap in diffuse at m = 0.5. The ambient/GI diffuse term has no Fresnel weight at all (`material_shading.h:257,261`, `reflection_composite.metal:110,114`) and the specular term is added on top (`:111`). In a furnace with albedo 1 a dielectric therefore returns more than 1.04. Ticket 03 still gates "p99 ≤ 1 %" against Appendix B and "no pixel > 1.005" (`issues/03:13-14`). Decision 06 then gives *extended* materials `diffuse × (1 − weight·max F)` (`decisions/06:28`). The same material would render differently with and without a default-valued `KHR_materials_specular`, which breaks the rule that extension defaults equal the core model. **Fix:** ticket 03 has to settle the base model explicitly. Option (a): keep it, declare the deviation, and gate the base furnace and grid only where the two models agree (m ∈ {0,1}, deviation table published). Option (b): an owner-approved base-correction ticket, which knowingly re-baselines visual_check, the F13/F14 corpus and D2. Either way, add "extension at defaults ≡ base, 0 px" to ticket 04.

**B2. Opaque transmissive shadows and GI contradict the Mitsuba gate.** In secondary hits, shadow rays and DDGI treat glass as opaque (`reflections.metal:54-71`), and so do probe captures (`:125-142`, ADR-0004:9). A bed seen through water, or a backdrop lit through glass, comes out dark in Phosphor and lit in Mitsuba. The shallow-water fixture (`issues/10:12`) and every through-glass-lit receiver under the 5 %/15 % gate (`:11`) fail by construction. **Fix:** gate only fixtures where nothing seen through glass is lit through glass (light on the camera side, receivers lit directly), and state this in decision 11 before measuring. Make shallow water qualitative, or move it to F16 together with coloured shadows.

## Major

**M1. Exit hits are rejected.** `reflectionMaterial` returns false for back-face hits on single-sided materials (`reflections.metal:21`). A refracted ray leaving a closed single-sided glass mesh therefore returns black. The CPU reference culls only primary rays (`rt_reference.cpp:196`). **Fix:** ticket 10 specifies interface traversal that accepts inside back faces of the transmissive medium. Add a fixture with a single-sided closed mesh and a negative control that keeps the current test.

**M2. Volumetric subsurface: the back-lit gate cannot pass, and the hybrid has no exit.** Screen-space Burley only blurs irradiance on the visible surface, so it cannot transmit light through thickness. The back-lit gate (`issues/12:11`) therefore fails for certain and triggers the hybrid, which has no threshold and no fallback of its own (METHOD:13-18). On top of that, Mitsuba's smooth ior-1.5 boundary must match the engine's rough GGX base. **Fix:** gate screen-space diffusion on side-lit fixtures only. Make the hybrid a separate ticket with its own bound, timebox and declared fallback (stays unticked). Freeze the boundary roughness used in Mitsuba.

**M3. The denoiser contract needs more than a roughness value.** The spatial axis and lobe weight use `reflect(-V,N)` (`denoise.metal:18-25`), and history is matched on `s.roughness` (`:41`). The à-trous filter mixes stochastic samples with no path test (`:117`), so Fresnel-chosen reflect and refract samples get averaged across neighbours. Writing the effective roughness into `GPUDISurface.roughness` would also shift the RT band, the GGX sampling and the probe LOD (`reflection_common.h:25,59`, `reflections.metal:100-104`). **Fix:** the lobe guide gives the denoiser a sampled-lobe id, axis (reflect/refract) and roughness through a new specialised binding. Neighbours and history are rejected across lobe ids. `GPUDISurface` stays untouched. Add a negative control: the reflect axis on glass must fail the clip.

**M4. The sheen environment term has nowhere to live in the product path.** With the transport on, the resolve omits ambient specular (`material_shading.h:262`, flag 8 at `visibility_resolve.metal:229`). The composite adds only GI·albedo·(1−m) plus the transport (`reflection_composite.metal:110-114`), yet decision 06 says sheen is "covered by environment/probe term". **Fix:** define that term in the composite (probe/GI radiance at N × Charlie albedo) and test it with a probe-lit fixture, not only a constant environment.

**M5. The indexed overflow fallback is not covered.** It writes the same guides through raster attachments (`visibility_renderer.cpp:69-75`, `RENDERING_F7_F8.md:51-53`). No ticket requires it to produce the lobe guide, extension reads or the subsurface irradiance target. The F13.1 overflow-emission defect is the precedent. **Fix:** tickets 05, 10 and 12 each require a forced-overflow capture equal to the meshlet path.

**M6. Turning a feature on or off at runtime is untested.** Changing the union means a new specialisation (fallback frames). Allocating the guide changes the graph key (transient realloc). More extension records grow the buffer, which hits O7 and O9. Ticket 04 tests parameter edits only (`issues/04:11`) against spec story 16. **Fix:** fixed per-material record capacity, or growth as a counted structure change. Define the all-lobes fallback while the specialisation compiles. Add a hitch test that toggles a feature.

**M7. The meaning of an undefined function constant is not stated.** The measured product default is the *generic* resolve with FC 21 undefined (`RENDERING_F7_F8.md:29-30`, `visibility_renderer.cpp:47`, `material_shading.h:14`). ADR-0003:10-11 nevertheless says generic is "never the product path". Sponza needs "undefined ⇒ no lobes", while the compile fallback needs "all lobes", and an added branch has already shifted contraction by 1 LSB (`material_shading.h:168-172`). **Fix:** fix the undefined value of the reserved constant. Keep Sponza's descriptors byte-identical (archive keys unchanged) and give the fallback an explicit all-lobes value. Correct ADR-0003.

**M8. Ticket structure.**
- 05 is far too large for one context: three producers (resolve, guides kernel, overflow) and many consumers (ReSTIR, clustered DI, reflection kernels, composite, three denoiser kernels). Split it into producer, DI and transport+denoiser tickets.
- The guide and extension-record layouts for *all* features must be frozen in 04/05 with static_asserts. Otherwise the "parallel" tickets 06–12 each change `gpu_types.h` and every consumer.
- 13 should not wait for 12. Only "transmission over diffuse transmission" needs 10+11. As it stands, 12's 2-week contingency sits on the critical path of 15–18.
- The binning rule needs both chess and terrain (`F15.md:66`, `decisions/08:36`), but 15 decides on chess only (`issues/15:14`) and terrain arrives in 17. Either move the decision to 17 or record it as provisional.

**M9. Relative thresholds have no absolute floor.** The transmission and subsurface bounds are relative luminance errors (`decisions/11:48-51`), so dark attenuated or shadowed pixels dominate the p95. The F13 closure adopted absolute-floor radiometry (D1). **Fix:** fix a floor and an ROI rule before the first comparison.
