## Defects corrected in this closure

Each fix is its own commit with its measurement in the message; the
[closure brief](../plans/F13-F14-CLOSURE-BRIEF-2026-11-10.md) holds the full
numbers per section.

- f30a93b, bae13c6, d9f1c82, 75c0b6e (F13.1): geometry emitters were
  counted twice between DI and RT reflections, then on the SSR, cache and
  probe fallbacks, then on indexed overflow receivers. One roughness band
  now decides ownership in every mode.
- f3d728e, bb376ab, 5e94b3e (F13.6): five different environments in one
  frame, the sun treated as a point in the primary specular term, and an
  unregularised light connection after a sampled lobe.
- c0527d6, ee21dce, dc6f3bb, aec6ddc (F13.4/F13.6): the stationary specular
  history is a running mean, the history survives geometry epochs with
  per-pixel checks, and k = 8 regularisation is the default (D5).
- 34ca3e2 (F13.2): GTAO slices collapsed onto one screen axis because
  `-p.height` wrapped as an unsigned value.
- be4ee1b, 609d124 (F13.5): the probe parallax box was the union of culling
  spheres (about 31 x 27 x 27 m for an 8 x 4 x 16 m room), the weight faded
  inside the box, and the split-sum DFG was 14% low at roughness 0.3-0.6.
- ef54033 (F14.3): the cloud history reset on every clock step instead of
  on discontinuities only.
- 699f5af (F9.4): float32 edge-critical rays are accepted only against the
  exact result without that triangle (D3).
